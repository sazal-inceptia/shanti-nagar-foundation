### Project 2: Pushti Nutrition Box Distribution for Pregnant Mothers (Phase 1)
- **Date:** 30 August 2025
- **Venue:** Azimpur Maternity Hospital, Dhaka
- **Expenditure:** ৳32,400.00 (12 packages @ ৳2,700)
- **Target Beneficiaries:** 20 underprivileged pregnant and lactating mothers
- **Key Objectives:**
  - Enhance maternal nutrition to prevent neonatal stunting and low birth weight.
