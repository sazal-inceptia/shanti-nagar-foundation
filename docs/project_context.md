## 2. Planned Modules & Backend Feature Roadmap

### A. Admin Dashboard & Authentication
- **Admin Panel Framework:** Filament / Custom Blade Dashboard / Tabler UI
- **Roles & Permissions:**
  - `Super Admin`: Full control over funds, settings, and users.
  - `Accountant / Finance Officer`: Manages donations, expenses, vouchers, and salary disbursements.
  - `Field Coordinator`: Manages project field status, photo uploads, and relief distributions.

### B. Payment Gateway Integrations (Future / Online Giving)
- **Target Local Gateways:**
  - bKash Merchant API (Tokenized / Checkout)
  - Nagad Payment Gateway
  - SSLCommerz / Shurjopay (for Card & Net Banking)
- **Manual / Offline Options:** Direct Bank Transfer slip uploads & Cash receipts at office counter.

### C. Public & Internal Reporting (Requirements #3 & #6)
- **Public Impact Summary:** Automated total donations collected vs. project expenditures.
- **Export Formats:** Monthly income-expense statement generation (PDF, Excel, CSV).
- **Salary History:** Payslip generator and monthly staff disbursement audit sheets.

## 3. UI/UX & Asset Guidelines
- **Color Palette:** Warm Orange (`#f65024` / `#ea580c`), Soft Emerald (`#03c0a8` / `#10b981`), Neutral Charcoal (`#232323`), Off-White background.
- **Typography:** Outfit / Quicksand for headings, Inter / Open Sans for body.
- **Bangladesh NGO Tone:** Respectful, transparent, community-centric, avoiding foreign corporate jargon.
